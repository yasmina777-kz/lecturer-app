QazSL: A Kazakh Sign Language Fingerspelling Dataset.
Nurbol Beisov, Vladimir Jotsov, Nurassyl Kerimbayev. Zenodo record 22830162.

Reference silhouettes in ref/*.png are resized, blurred frames from this dataset.
Paper: Technologies 2026, 14(11), 744, https://doi.org/10.3390/technologies14110744

Bukva: Russian Sign Language Alphabet Dataset (ai-forever, 2024), https://github.com/ai-forever/bukva
Landmark tracks of its 33 letters shared with the Kazakh alphabet were drawn as
silhouettes for training. Model weights are therefore distributed under the Bukva
licence terms as well (see /research/rsl/bukva-license.pdf).

«Саусақтар сөйлейді» — free Kazakh sign language course by «Жас Өрен» youth centre (Aktobe),
YouTube channel Sausaqtarsoileidi: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCmKNkh-WKUBVKU19atCGyNQ
Hand landmarks were extracted from frames of 37 letter lessons marked correct (green check).
The videos are standard-licence YouTube content and are NOT redistributed; only derived
landmark-based model weights are. Remove on the rights holder's request.
