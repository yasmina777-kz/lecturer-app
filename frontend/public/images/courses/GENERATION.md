# Course covers

Generated with Figma AI (gpt-image-2.5-flare), resized to 1200px WebP. Decorative learning scenes, not instructional sign diagrams.

- `rsl.webp`: young Central Asian woman at a pale desk practising fingerspelling in front of a laptop; cobalt / ice-blue / ivory palette.
- `ksl.webp`: two young Kazakh adults signing in a bright Almaty cafe, soft Kazakh ornament on the wall; teal / ivory / gold palette.

# Kazakh SL topic tiles (`../ksl-topics/01–27.webp`)

Same tool. Three 3×3 sheets of realistic editorial photographs (adult, muted ivory / beige / teal / slate grading), one per SURDO.KZ topic in the course ORDER (questions, family, numbers … religion), sliced along the white gutters and resized to 640×360 WebP. An earlier clay-illustration set was replaced as too childish.

# RSL course unit photos (`../rsl-units/01–54.webp`)

Same tool and look as the KSL topic tiles: six 3×3 sheets of realistic editorial photos, sliced along the gutters to 640×360 WebP. Tiles 1–48 follow the unit order A1 (1–16), A2 (101–120), B1 (201–212); tiles 49–54 are extra topic photos for the RSL word trainer (actions, objects, everyday, health, nature, family). They replace the cartoon `illustrations/sign-phrases` scenes on unit and topic cards.
